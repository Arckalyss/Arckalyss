## Featured projects

### 🩺 [ExpMedIA](https://github.com/Arckalyss/ExpMedIA)
Systematic evaluation of Integrated Gradients, SHAP, and LIME on four BERT variants fine-tuned for diabetes detection on MIMIC-III clinical notes — faithfulness metrics, cross-method agreement, and a case study on models missing negation.

### 🏥 [AIME](https://github.com/Arckalyss/aime)
NLP pipeline (DrBERT + BioLORD) built with a 5-person team and the psychiatry department of a partner hospital, to help clinicians map hospitalization reports to ICD-10 codes.

### ⚖️ [LoRA](https://github.com/Arckalyss/LoRA)
Hands-on demonstrators exploring LoRA/DoRA fine-tuning and LLM guardrail architectures through the lens of the EU AI Act (substantial modification, risk management, traceability).
